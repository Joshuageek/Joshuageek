Requirements

Therapist Requirements

  • An active, independent US state board-approved clinical license
  • Individual professional malpractice liability insurance policy
  • Submission of a fully completed, signed CAQH application
  • Individual NPI number
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Background check (conducted by a third party)
  • Current residence in the USA

Associate Therapist Requirements

  • An active, associate clinical license
  • Master's Degree in social work, counseling, or behavioral science
  • A supervising clinician and/or organization
  • Individual professional malpractice liability insurance policy
  • Reliable internet connection
  • Background check (conducted by a third party)
  • Current residence in the USA
